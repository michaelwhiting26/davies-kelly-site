Dispute resolution

Prevention is better than cure, but claims for time and money often arise during construction and too often become disputes. We help clients identify and resolve them, before and after they arise.

What we do

We act for employers, main contractors and subcontractors in preparing and defending extension of time and cost claims, and we help parties resolve claims through impartial third-party advice and determinations.

Our expertise

  • Claim preparation
  • Claim defence
  • Independent determinations

Example

For a UAE main contractor we built a consolidated extension of time and cost claim from first principles, focused on the issues with the greatest impact, and managed it through to the engineer’s determination. The contractor was awarded an extension of time of about a year and around USD 10 million.

Advice and evidence are different roles

When we advise on a claim we work in your interest. If we are later asked to act as the independent expert in the same dispute, we tell you at the outset, because an expert’s duty is to the tribunal.
