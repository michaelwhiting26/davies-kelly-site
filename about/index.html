About Davies Kelly

Davies Kelly is a Dubai firm of construction experts, founded in 2020 by Michael Roos and Jamie Shafto, a quantum expert and a delay expert. We give independent expert evidence in construction and engineering disputes and advise on claims before and during a dispute. We have been appointed as independent experts and advisers by international law firms, contractors and developers, in matters from mediation to international arbitration.

Why a small firm

The expert does the work. The person named in the report is the person who analysed the records.

Fewer conflicts. A small firm with a focused practice is less often conflicted on large projects. We tell you the result of our conflict check before you send documents.

Built on site experience. Our experts started as a labourer, a planner, an engineer and an apprentice quantity surveyor. They value and analyse claims the way projects are actually built.

What we stand for

  1. Our duty as experts is to the tribunal.
  2. We say what the evidence supports, including when it is not what the instructing party hoped to hear.
  3. We use recognised methods and show our working.
  4. We state our assumptions and the limits of what we were given.
  5. Senior people do the work.
  6. We keep our clients’ matters confidential.
