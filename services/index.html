Services

Davies Kelly is an international construction consultancy providing pre-contract advice, project advisory, dispute resolution and expert witness services. We give independent expert evidence on quantum, delay and engineering issues, and we advise on claims before and during a dispute. Expert and adviser are different roles with different duties, so we keep them apart.

Pre-contract advice

Review and drafting of construction contracts and professional services agreements, and identification of contractual risk before you sign.

Two roles, kept separate

As advisers we work in your interest. As experts our duty is to the tribunal. We tell you at the outset which role we are in, and we do not switch roles in the same dispute without telling everyone affected.
